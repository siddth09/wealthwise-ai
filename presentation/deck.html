💡 The Idea

WealthWise AI is an AI-powered digital wealth advisor with an animated avatar that integrates seamlessly into a bank's mobile application. It transforms how customers interact with their finances by combining behavioral finance analysis, goal-based planning, and personalized recommendations — all through a conversational AI interface.

🎯 The Problem

Wealth advisory is fragmented, expensive, and inaccessible. Most customers get generic advice that doesn't account for their actual spending behavior or life goals.

🚀 Our Approach

An AI avatar that knows your finances intimately — your spending patterns, investment behavior, risk appetite — and proactively guides you to better financial decisions in real-time.

🏆 The Impact

Democratize wealth management for millions of bank customers, increasing engagement by 5x while reducing cost-per-customer by 90% compared to human advisors.

03 / 13

📊 Opportunities & USP

How WealthWise AI stands apart from existing solutions

  • 1
    Avatar-Based Interaction — Unlike text-only robo-advisors (Zerodha Coin, Groww), WealthWise has an animated AI avatar that speaks and engages naturally using Web Speech API, creating an emotional connection.
  • 2
    Behavioral Finance Engine — Goes beyond transaction tracking. Detects psychological spending patterns (impulse buying, retail therapy, loss aversion) and provides behavioral nudges.
  • 3
    Unified Platform — Combines portfolio management + spending analysis + goal planning + AI advisory in one seamless mobile-first interface, unlike fragmented tools.
  • 4
    Gamified Health Score — A unique 0-100 Financial Health Score with weighted breakdown, making financial wellness engaging and trackable.
  • 5
    Bank-Integration Ready — Designed as a PWA that plugs into existing banking apps via Account Aggregator framework, with zero friction for end users.
